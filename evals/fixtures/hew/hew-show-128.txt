### Where

`hew show`; `internal/render`; `internal/cli`

### Problem

`hew show` is the deliberate inspection path for a specific issue, including untriaged reports. Invisible Unicode and confusable characters can conceal content in its title, body, or displayed comments. Triage annotations do not cover comments or accompany a direct detail read.

### Approach

Reuse the pure Unicode scanner introduced by #100 to annotate suspicious content in `hew show`, preserving its emoji exceptions. Identify each finding as belonging to the title, body, or a specific displayed comment. Provide equivalent source information in `--json`. Keep findings advisory and preserve existing text sanitization and issue visibility. Limit this follow-up to the detail view.

### Done when

- [ ] `hew show` reports zero-width characters, bidi controls, Unicode tags, and confusables in the title, body, and displayed comment bodies.
- [ ] Each finding identifies the affected field or displayed comment in both text and JSON output.
- [ ] Clean text and recognized emoji produce no findings; suspicious content remains inspectable under the existing rendering rules.
- [ ] Tests cover title, body, and comment findings, clean content, both output formats, and the existing capped-comment behavior.
- [ ] Documentation explains the detail-view annotations; automatic commands retain their current behavior.
